About FavourNova Supplies

FavourNova Supplies is a growing retail and wholesale business providing a wide range of quality products, including electronics, office equipment, personal care products, food and beverages, and other everyday essentials.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality products while maintaining accurate inventory records, efficient supplier management, reliable customer service, and informed business decisions.

What We Do

Manage products and inventory
Manage suppliers and purchases
Manage customers
Record and track sales
Monitor business performance
Track stock levels and low-stock products
Generate sales invoices
Analyze best-selling products and revenue

About Our Inventory System

FavourNova Supplies uses this Inventory Management System to manage daily inventory and business operations from one centralized platform. The system helps us keep track of products, suppliers, purchases, customers, sales, stock levels, invoices, and business reports.

About This Project

This Inventory Management System is a full-stack web application developed using Flask, SQLite, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
